# Strand: IoT verification and behavioral comparison

**Goal:** separate fixed-property verification from per-request implementation equivalence.

## Scope

1. Model checking of IoT safety and interaction properties.
2. Synthesis/repair against temporal properties.
3. Reference artifact, checked property, counterexample, and guarantee boundary.

## Per-entry deliverable

Each entry has `card.md`, `source.md`, `meta.json`; the strand has `INDEX.md` and a BibTeX file.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 2 systems with explicit formal models and checked properties.
- Every card records why its verifier does or does not answer OVLA's per-request IR–code question.

## Out of scope

- Security scanners without behavioral or temporal models.
