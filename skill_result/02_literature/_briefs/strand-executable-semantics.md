# Strand: Executable deterministic semantics

**Goal:** ground the use of explicit time, synchronous reactions, deterministic concurrency, and executable formal models.

## Scope

1. Timed transition systems.
2. Synchronous languages and deterministic concurrency.
3. Operational semantics as executable reference behavior.

## Per-entry deliverable

Each entry has `card.md`, `source.md`, `meta.json`; the strand has `INDEX.md` and a BibTeX file.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 2 foundational sources.
- Cards distinguish general semantic foundations from OVLA-specific novelty.

## Out of scope

- Claiming that adopting established semantic concepts is itself novel.
