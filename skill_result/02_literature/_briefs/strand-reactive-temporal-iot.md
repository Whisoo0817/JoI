# Strand: Reactive-temporal IoT models

**Goal:** establish how trigger-action and concurrent IoT work model events, timing, state, interactions, and user-authored behavior.

## Scope

1. Trigger-action end-user programming semantics and expressiveness.
2. Concurrent, delayed, and physical-world interactions.
3. Boundary between a user-authored rule and a separately generated implementation.

## Per-entry deliverable

Each entry has `card.md`, `source.md`, `meta.json`; the strand has `INDEX.md` and a BibTeX file.

## Seed material

- S3 §2, §5.1, §7, §8.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 2 primary works covering user-authored TAP and concurrent/timed semantics.
- Cards state whether the rule itself is the specification or a generated artifact is checked against it.

## Out of scope

- General-purpose IoT middleware with no automation semantics.
