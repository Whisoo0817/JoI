# Strand: Reachability and bounded guarantees

**Goal:** ground reachable-state exploration, counterexample search, and precise boundedness language.

## Scope

1. Explicit-state model checking.
2. Bounded model checking and counterexample depth.
3. State explosion and outcome accounting.

## Per-entry deliverable

Each entry has `card.md`, `source.md`, `meta.json`; the strand has `INDEX.md` and a BibTeX file.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 2 foundational sources with different exploration styles.
- Cards explicitly state what completion within a bound does and does not establish.

## Out of scope

- Introducing partial-order reduction or abstraction into the core design without approval.
