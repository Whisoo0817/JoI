# Strand: User-confirmed specifications

**Goal:** document systems that expose automation intent/properties to users and clarify what user confirmation validates.

## Scope

1. Interactive confirmation and refinement of generated automation.
2. Property interfaces for trigger-action programs.
3. Distinction between authoring/usability evidence and implementation-verification evidence.

## Per-entry deliverable

Each entry has `card.md`, `source.md`, `meta.json`; the strand has `INDEX.md` and a BibTeX file.

## Acceptance criteria

- At least 2 primary works.
- Cards identify whether confirmation correctness was empirically studied.

## Out of scope

- Reintroducing a usability claim into new OVLA.
