# OCTOPINF (PerCom 2025)

- **Focus:** workload-aware real-time inference serving for edge video analytics.
- **Artifact/evidence:** nine Jetson devices plus four GPUs, nine 13-hour real videos, three baselines, high-load robustness tests, ablation, and long-running operation.
- **Transfer to OVLA:** couple the synthetic state-space sweep with a real-workload anchor; include stress/frontier cases and isolate the component responsible for the claimed behavior.
- **Venue verification:** official 2025 accepted-paper list.
- **Content source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01277
