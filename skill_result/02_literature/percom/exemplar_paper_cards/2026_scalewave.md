# ScaleWave (PerCom 2026)

- **Focus:** workload-aware scaling across heterogeneous edge resources.
- **Artifact/evidence:** motivating trace study; laptop, ProDesk, Raspberry Pi, and Jetson testbed; real workload trace; comparisons with Knative-based baselines.
- **Metrics:** throughput, latency, and resource consumption; threats to validity are stated.
- **Transfer to OVLA:** motivate the bottleneck with measured behavior, retain a real-workload anchor, and expose heterogeneous edge cost if claiming deployability.
- **Source:** https://people.cs.vt.edu/~tilevich/papers/PERCOM2026.pdf
