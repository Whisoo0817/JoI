# SenseLess (PerCom 2026)

- **Focus:** sensing with reduced sensor dependence in smart homes.
- **Artifact/evidence:** four-week real-home data, labeling baselines, delay-compensation ablation, six-day deployment, and cross-home validation.
- **Reporting:** deployment evidence is paired with explicit limitations rather than generalized beyond the sampled homes.
- **Transfer to OVLA:** independently sourced home-automation cases and stratified cross-context checks would strengthen representational adequacy; report unsupported cases.
- **Source:** https://iotgarage.net/publications/pdfs/Albazzai2026a.pdf
