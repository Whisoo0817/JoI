# PSMark (PerCom 2026)

- **Focus:** benchmark for pervasive publish/subscribe middleware.
- **Artifact/evidence:** 12 workloads from seven real datasets across four domains; five MQTT systems and one DDS system; commodity single-node and 2/5/8-node cluster conditions; low/high QoS.
- **Metrics and accounting:** latency, throughput, message drops, CPU/memory/network; seven repetitions for multi-node and four for single-node; unsupported configurations retained as N/A.
- **Transfer to OVLA:** publish a frozen workload taxonomy, preserve unsupported/error/timeout outcomes, and report distribution/tail behavior rather than one aggregate.
- **Source:** https://mdsoar.org/items/0b4ebbdf-94c1-4509-ab1f-6b9bb414a996
