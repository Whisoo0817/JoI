# Teaching Things To Think (PerCom 2025)

- **Published title:** *Teaching Things To Think: Bootstrapping Local Reasoning for Smart(er) Devices*; the preprint is titled *Thoughtful Things*.
- **Artifact/evidence:** lamp and thermostat prototypes on Raspberry Pi; explicit research questions; two small language models; held-out cases; human validation of GPT-derived labels; quantization, runtime, and limitations.
- **Transfer to OVLA:** use independent validation when an automated oracle contributes labels; demonstrate local/edge feasibility only as a performance property, not physical correctness.
- **Venue verification:** https://percom.org/2025/accepted-papers-main-conference/
- **Content source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.03821
