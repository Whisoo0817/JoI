# PerCom 2027 official scope and requirements

Verified on 2026-09-04 from the official PerCom site.

## Venue fit

- PerCom explicitly includes foundational theory and modeling for pervasive computing, including programming paradigms and spatio-temporal modeling.
- It explicitly includes smart homes, pervasive virtual assistants, IoT/sensor systems, AI, and cyber-physical systems.
- Contributions may be analytical, empirical, technological, methodological, or mixed.
- The call strongly encourages systems-engineering contributions with solid, appropriate evaluation.
- Novelty, significant impact in a pervasive-computing application context, and rigorous validation are required.

New OVLA is therefore in scope if the paper continually anchors the semantics and verification contribution to deployable smart-home/pervasive automation rather than presenting it as a context-free PL/formal-methods paper.

## Submission constraints

| Item | Official requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Conference | March 2027, Goa, India; in-person physical event |
| Review | Double blind; prior self-work cited in third person |
| Main paper length | At most 9 pages of technical content in 10pt, two-column IEEE format |
| References | Up to 1 additional references-only page |
| Registration | 2026-09-04 AoE |
| Submission | 2026-09-11 AoE |
| Initial outcome | 2026-11-20: early rejection or rebuttal invitation |
| Rebuttal | 2026-11-27; clarification-focused, additional experiments not expected |
| Notification | 2026-12-18 |
| Presentation | Accepted papers require full registration and in-person presentation |

Desk-rejection risks named by the call include incompleteness, anonymity violations, formatting/page violations, out-of-scope work, and immature work lacking adequate evaluation.

## Artifact status

The paper call says accepted papers may submit an artifact and that artifact presence is considered for best-paper selection. The dedicated 2027 artifact page currently says only “Will be announced soon.” Therefore this plan recommends artifact-ready organization but does not invent 2027 packaging or badge requirements.
