# PerCom exemplar selection protocol

## Question

What recurring presentation and evaluation traits appear in recent PerCom main-conference full papers that are relevant to new OVLA?

## Sampling rule

- Venue: IEEE PerCom main conference only, not workshops, WiP, demo, industry, or short-paper tracks.
- Years: 2024–2026, emphasizing the two most recent completed editions.
- Size: seven full papers.
- Relevance strata: smart-device/IoT behavior; edge or pervasive systems; benchmarks; on-device AI; real deployment.
- Sources: official accepted-program pages establish venue status; DOI, author, repository, or arXiv copies establish paper content.

## Selected sample

| Year | Paper | Selection reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | PSMark | pervasive messaging benchmark with heterogeneous testbed and explicit outcome accounting |
| 2026 | ScaleWave | edge systems mechanism motivated by real traces and tested on heterogeneous devices |
| 2026 | SenseLess | longitudinal in-home sensing with cross-home and ablation evidence |
| 2025 | EcoVis | real testbed, energy/connectivity trade-offs, and multiple baselines |
| 2025 | OCTOPINF | long-running real edge-video workloads, baselines, robustness, and ablation |
| 2025 | Teaching Things To Think | local reasoning on smart devices, task questions, held-out tests, human label validation, and runtime/resource evidence |
| 2024 | DiTMoS | MCU deployment measurements, repeated runs, datasets, baselines, and ablation |

## Extraction fields

Each card records problem framing, artifact, evaluation environment, baselines/controls, metrics, robustness/failure reporting, limitations, and the transferable lesson for OVLA.

## Limits

This is purposive sampling, not a statistical sample of all accepted papers. A recurring trait is reported as an **observed tendency**, never as an official acceptance rule. Paper quality and acceptance causes cannot be inferred from accepted papers alone.
