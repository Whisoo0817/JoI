# TL;DR

Synchronous languages show how logically simultaneous reactions can retain deterministic semantics through disciplined concurrency and compilation.

## Relevance to new OVLA

The survey supports a snapshot–reaction–commit design, explicit causality, and rejection or resolution of instantaneous conflicts. These are design precedents rather than novelty.

## Boundary

It does not address LLM-generated IoT programs or equivalence to a per-request, user-confirmed Timeline IR.
