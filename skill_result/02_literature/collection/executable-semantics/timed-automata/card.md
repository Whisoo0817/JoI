# TL;DR

Timed automata establish a standard finite-state-plus-clock foundation for reasoning about real-time behavior. They are method provenance, not an OVLA novelty claim.

## Relevance to new OVLA

The paper motivates making time domains, clock comparisons, transitions, and reachability bounds explicit. New OVLA should use a smaller discrete-time operational model unless continuous-time expressiveness is actually required.

## Boundary

The foundation does not supply a user-confirmed executable reference, generated-code adapter, or IR–code observational-equivalence workflow.
