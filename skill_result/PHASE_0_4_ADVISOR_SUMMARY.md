## PerCom grounding

The official 2027 call directly covers smart homes, pervasive virtual assistants, IoT/AI intersections, programming paradigms, and spatio-temporal modeling, while requiring significant pervasive impact and rigorous validation. The seven-paper 2024–2026 sample suggests—without making it an official rule—that successful systems narratives often combine a concrete pervasive context, runnable artifact, real-workload anchor, controlled coverage, relevant controls, multiple/tail/resource metrics, and explicit failure/limitation reporting.

These tendencies were folded inside the existing blocks: real/external automations in E2, genuine and held-out faults in E3, real-workload and possible edge-hardware subconditions in E4, and full error/timeout accounting everywhere.

## Four core experiment blocks

1. **E1 Interpreter conformance:** independent semantics-derived oracle, property generation, boundary/permutation/merge tests, exhaustive small-model successor checks.
2. **E2 Expressive adequacy:** frozen taxonomy, provenance-aware legacy cases, independent external corpus, unsupported controls, two annotations plus adjudication.
3. **E3 IR–code equivalence:** syntax-different positives, genuine LLM failures, held-out non-IR faults, mutants, exact labels, false accept/reject and completion accounting.
4. **E4 Explorer scalability:** factorial state/event/timer/rule/depth/horizon sweep, real anchors, exact completion classes, time/RSS/frontier and tail metrics.

No experiment has been run. `claim-map.json` and `run-blocks.json` are the machine-readable planning authority.
