# Evaluation — SenSys version

The SenSys evaluation contained four research questions: deterministic rendering faithfulness, verifier fault detection, end-to-end system safety through generation and repair, and on-device cost and deployment. It used 382 natural-language automations, injected mutations, local quantized generators, dense randomized replay, and physical-device experiments. The manuscript reported 99.3% detection over 1,552 faults and sub-millisecond median gate latency.

These experiments answer the earlier boundary-event and deployment framing. Their numbers and denominators do not automatically support the redesigned Timeline adequacy, validation-fidelity, generated-candidate application, and Explorer-cost questions.
