# E1-072 — home and away lighting

Source: Google Home official scripted-automation example, “Home and away lighting.”

## Frozen interpretation

On transition to HOME, turn on `light1` and `light2` only between sunset and sunrise. On transition to AWAY, turn off `light1`, `light2`, and `light3` only between sunrise and sunset. No action is inferred in the complementary time range.

## Binding plan

- Inputs: `HomePresence ∈ {HOME, AWAY}` and supplied sunrise/sunset clock events.
- Actions: `Lights.On(light1, light2)`, `Lights.Off(light1, light2, light3)`.

## Reference history and expected trace

For this history, sunrise is 06:00 and sunset is 18:00.

| 20:00 | presence becomes AWAY | -- |
