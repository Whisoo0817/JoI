# E1-095 — internal post-start accumulation

Source: Home Assistant Community thread 654561, “Getting an average reading of a sensor during certain hours.”

## Frozen interpretation

Every day, read pH and chlorine at 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, and 14:00. At 15:00 report the arithmetic mean of the five readings for each sensor. The primary version performs internal `sum += value` and `count += 1`; it must not delegate averaging to a history or statistics service.

## Binding plan

- Inputs: clock times and `Pool.PH`, `Pool.Chlorine` values.
- Action: `Pool.ReportDailyQuality(meanPH, meanChlorine)`.

| Time | Input values | Expected action |
|---|---|---|
| 10:00 | pH=7.2, chlorine=1.0 | -- |
| 11:00 | pH=7.4, chlorine=1.2 | -- |

All five samples are present; missing-sample behavior is outside this bounded case.
